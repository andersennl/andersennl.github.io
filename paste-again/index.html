macOS app

Paste Again

Everything you copy stays within reach. Hit one shortcut, start typing, press Enter — the text lands right back where you were working.

Searchable history

Every piece of text you copy is recorded in the background and stays fuzzy-searchable.

One shortcut away

A global shortcut opens the panel from any app — ⇧⌘V by default, reassignable in settings.

Pastes back for you

Pick an entry and it is pasted straight into the app you came from — no window juggling.

Keyboard-first

Arrow keys to browse, ⌘C to copy without pasting, Esc to dismiss. The mouse is optional.

Out of the way

Lives in the menu bar, starts at login if you want, and pauses whenever you copy something private.

Under 2 MB

The entire app is a single 1.4 MB binary built against Apple’s own frameworks — nothing else is bundled.

Your clipboard stays yours

Stored locally on your Mac. No account, no sync, no analytics. Content marked confidential by password managers is never recorded. Privacy policy
