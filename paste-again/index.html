Paste Again

Everything you copy stays within reach. Hit one shortcut, start typing, press Enter — the text lands right back where you were working.

What it does

  • Searchable history Every piece of text you copy is recorded in the background and stays fuzzy-searchable.
  • One shortcut away A global shortcut opens the panel from any app. ⇧⌘V by default, reassignable in settings.
  • Pastes back for you Pick an entry and it is pasted straight into the app you came from — no window juggling.
  • Keyboard-first Arrow keys to browse, ⌘C to copy without pasting, Esc to dismiss. The mouse is optional.
  • Out of the way Lives in the menu bar, can start at login if you want it to, and can be paused whenever you copy something private.

Your clipboard stays yours

The history is stored locally on your Mac. There is no account, no sync, and no analytics — nothing you copy ever leaves the device. Content that password managers mark as confidential is never recorded. Read the privacy policy.
