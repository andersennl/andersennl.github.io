Lactase 3

Lactase 3 is being carefully rebuilt with current Apple frameworks. The present TestFlight build is for internal product review.

About the internal test

The historical calculation screen reproduces the behavior of the 2016 release for comparison. It includes known portion and ounce-conversion defects and is not dosing advice.

  • The BLS 4.0 food entries are visible as review candidates only.
  • Candidate food values are not installed as production data and cannot be used in the historical calculation.
  • The calculation test accepts only foods that the tester creates locally.
  • The app is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
