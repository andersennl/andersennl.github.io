Lactase for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective: July 29, 2026

Lactase processes data locally on your device. The current app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on the device

The app may store the information you enter, including user display names, custom food names, lactose values, and app settings. This data remains in the app’s local SwiftData database on your iPhone or iPad.

If an older 2016 installation left a Realm database on the device, the rebuilt app does not open, import, modify, or delete that file.

No collection or tracking

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDK is included.
  • No app data is uploaded to a developer server.
  • No data is used for advertising or linked across apps and websites.

Your control

You can edit or delete custom foods and additional users in the app. Deleting the app removes its current local container from the device according to iOS behavior. TestFlight and Apple may separately process diagnostic or account information under Apple’s own policies.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before introducing any feature that changes data handling. Questions can be sent to andersen.nikolaj@icloud.com.
